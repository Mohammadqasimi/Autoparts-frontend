Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

AFJ Auto Parts & Wrecker ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through afjautoparts.com.au, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you use our contact forms, "Get a Quote" form, or "Sell Your Car" form, we may collect:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your email address
  • Details about the vehicle or part you're enquiring about (make, model, year, condition)
  • Any message or additional details you choose to provide

We do not collect payment card details through this website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote requests and enquiries
  • To arrange purchase of your vehicle if you submit a "Sell Your Car" request
  • To send you information you've requested, such as newsletter updates, if you opt in
  • To improve our website and service

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

How We Store Your Information

Form submissions are sent directly to our business email and are not stored in a public database on this website. We take reasonable steps to protect this information from unauthorised access.

Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies or similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (such as email delivery providers) to process form submissions. These providers only receive the information necessary to deliver your enquiry to us.

Your Rights

You may ask us at any time to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at info@afjautoparts.com.au or via the contact details on our Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
